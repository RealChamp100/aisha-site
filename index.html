What looks like a people problem is often a system problem.

The challenges you've identified may look separate. But they can be different symptoms of the same underlying issue: the system shaping how work gets done.

I help organisations redesign the systems that produce their culture.

The people, principles and processes in your organisation shape how work gets done and what employees and customers experience.

My starting question is simple:

"Does this serve everyone it's supposed to serve?"

That question helps identify what is working, what is getting in the way and where change will have the greatest impact.

The problems we can solve together

Three common organisational challenges.

The same underlying system problems can show up in different ways. These are three challenges I commonly help organisations address.

People

Progress depends on people you don't manage.

People are expected to move work forward but rely on colleagues they do not manage, making it difficult to build commitment, influence others and maintain momentum without being able to simply tell people what to do.

Principles

Capable teams are pulling in different directions.

Different assumptions about decisions, ownership, communication and accountability create delay, duplication and frustration.

Processes

Processes produce inconsistent experiences.

A process may appear to work overall while creating friction or very different outcomes for the employees or customers using it.

How I help address these challenges

Culture is a system. Systems can be designed.

Leaders can set a new direction and make it clear what needs to change. But if the system around people continues to reinforce the old way of doing things, the change is unlikely to stay.

Ways of Working Workshop
Create clarity on how your team works together.

When expectations aren't defined, capable people make different assumptions about decisions, ownership, communication and accountability.

The problem

Teams lose time and effort because the unwritten rules are different for everyone.

What we do

Create shared agreements for how the team communicates, makes decisions, handles disagreement, raises problems and holds each other accountable.

Outcomes

  • Clearer ownership and accountability
  • Better communication and collaboration
  • Clearer, more consistent execution

Ideal for

New leadership teams, restructures and mergers, cross-functional teams, or established teams where capable people are pulling in different directions.

Processes
Process Redesign Workshop
Design a better, more consistent experience.

A process can appear to work overall while creating very different experiences for the employees or customers using it.

The problem

Assumptions, friction and barriers can stay hidden until you look at the process through the experience of the people using it.

What we do

Identify where the process is falling short, understand why and agree on practical improvements with the people closest to the work.

Outcomes

  • A clear view of where the process creates friction and for whom
  • Better understanding of what is causing the friction
  • Practical, agreed improvements ready to take forward

Ideal for

Teams responsible for employee or customer experience, service design or organisational processes.
